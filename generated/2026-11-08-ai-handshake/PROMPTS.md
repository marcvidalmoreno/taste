# Prompt pack: ai-handshake

For each style: open a new Gemini chat (https://gemini.google.com/app), choose image generation,
drag in the 3 images from `refs/<style>/`, paste the prompt, then save the result here as `<style>.png`.

```text

Style: High-key, pale scenes: white rooms, pale ukiyo-e, sunlit beaches and light flat illustrations with big calm areas of colour.
Light: bright diffuse daylight, almost shadowless, gentle warm highlights; exposed high key
Composition: lots of white or pastel space, horizon or window line placed low, one small subject or object, symmetric or grid-like
Medium: clean interior or landscape photography, or flat pastel illustration and ink-wash drawing
Mood: light, tidy, breathable, optimistic but not cute
Texture and detail: smooth surfaces, soft paper or plaster texture, very few objects, thin line detail
Colour palette: #e2e1da, #c1b8aa, #8a7f76, #548ec5, #4a3c37, #d58a5a, #f4d9a0
Use the attached images only as style references (colour, light, texture, composition), never copy their content.
Avoid: dark vignettes, saturated neon, heavy contrast, busy patterns, stock-photo office people, cartoon outlines in thick black, text and logos. No text, no logos, no watermark.
```
